

# 🏏 T20 World Cup Match Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project delivers an **interactive analytical dashboard** for **T20 World Cup cricket data**, focusing on **team performance, run comparisons, match stages, venues, and winning trends**.
The dashboard is designed to demonstrate **sports analytics, data storytelling, and visualization skills** using match-level data.

This project is ideal for:

* Tableau / Power BI portfolio
* Sports analytics projects
* Academic submissions
* Interview demonstrations

---

## 📊 Dashboards & Visuals Included

## 1️⃣ Team Performance & Comparison

### 🔹 Team1 vs Team2 Run Comparison

* Bar chart comparing **total runs scored by Team 1 and Team 2**
* Teams include:

  * India, Pakistan, Australia, England, South Africa, New Zealand, etc.
* Helps analyze **batting strength and consistency**

### 🔹 Run Difference Distribution (Histogram)

* Shows frequency distribution of **run differences**
* Helps understand:

  * Close matches
  * One-sided wins
  * Competitive balance

---

## 2️⃣ Team Strength & Hierarchy Analysis

### 🔹 Group Teams into Strong vs Weak (Pie Chart)

* Classifies teams based on total runs / wins
* Visual comparison of **dominant vs underperforming teams**

### 🔹 Create Hierarchy – Stage-wise Total Runs

* Hierarchical analysis by:

  * Match Stage → Total Runs
* Stages included:

  * Group
  * Super 8
  * Semi Final
  * Final

---

## 3️⃣ Venue & Stage Performance

### 🔹 Venue-wise Average Runs

* Bar chart showing average runs scored at venues such as:

  * Florida
  * St Lucia
  * New York
  * Barbados
  * Guyana
  * Dallas
  * Trinidad
  * Antigua
* Helps identify **high-scoring and low-scoring grounds**

### 🔹 Stage-wise Winning Trend (Line Chart)

* Shows number of matches won across different stages
* Useful for analyzing **pressure performance in knockouts**

---

## 4️⃣ Match Outcome & Winning Analysis

### 🔹 Stage-wise Total Runs Analysis

* Compares total runs scored in:

  * Group stage
  * Super 8
  * Semi Final
  * Final
* Highlights how scoring patterns change as tournament progresses

### 🔹 Top 10 Winning Teams by Matches Won

* Ranks teams based on **number of matches won**
* Identifies **most successful teams in the tournament**
